Urban Metamorphosis — User Study

Study: Evaluating day-to-night city transition videos generated by AI

Instructions

You will watch 10 pairs of short videos. Each pair shows the same scene generated by two different AI models. Please rate each video on a scale of 1–5 based on:

  1. Visual quality: How realistic and clear is the video?
  2. Temporal smoothness: Is the transition smooth or flickering?
  3. Semantic alignment: Does it clearly show day→night?
  4. Overall impression: How engaging is it?
